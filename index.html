Eldeco · Terra & Sol · Sector 80, Gurugram

Go-to-market plan for the promoter

The opportunity

Terra & Sol is the lowest-priced launch in Sector 80, and investors can check the arithmetic themselves.

At ₹13,500 a sq ft it is 12% under the median of Ashiana Aaroham, Emaar Serenity Hills and Conscient Elaira, and within 4% of the newest similar ready flats. In the same sector, Ashiana is already selling its final phase. The investor case is real. The job is one price, one plan, and ads that buy site visits.

The risk

Buyers are being sold five prices and four payment plans. Live ads start Terra & Sol anywhere from ₹2.85 Cr to ₹3.33 Cr. Eldeco's own page offers "10% now, nothing for 36 months", while the price record says 30% within 30 days. At least 11 advertisers on Meta and eight look-alike websites on Google are selling it.

But NCR has slowed. NCR sales fell 11% in Jan–Sep 2026 (Knight Frank), and unsold stock is 18 months of sales (ANAROCK, Q3 2026). That is why this forecast is very conservative.
Investors are the buyers right now. The workbook's flip test passes: at possession, Terra & Sol's carried cost would sit about 16% above similar ready stock grown at 7% a year, inside the 25% flip band. Present it as an expectation with its date, never as a return.

Where Terra & Sol sits on price.

Below every launch nearby

₹13,500 against ₹14,500–15,926 for Conscient Elaira, Emaar Serenity Hills, Ashiana Aaroham and Eldeco's own Fairway Reserve. The workbook's strongest claim.

A second price is in the ads

Eldeco's own ads say "from ₹3.33 Cr", about ₹15,500 a sq ft on the 2,150. If that is the scheme price, the 12% gap closes. Settle it before spending.

Rent offsets, never income

At possession, yields run ~1.7–2.0% gross and ~1.1% net, and rent covers about 28–32% of the EMI. Never quote a yield.

Why the money goes where it goes.

Every rupee is media

No events, hoardings or partner activation. Creative, landing pages and CRM sit in Fixit's fee, and brokerage is paid separately. Partner sales outside these ads come on top of the forecast.

Investors in nearby cities first

Delhi and Gurugram take 49% of the budget. The workbook's phase grid says post-launch buyers are investors; end-user ads wait for the 2029 superstructure.

Nothing abroad before May 2027

Three of four overseas pull factors fail. The Gulf opens at Sustenance, and then only for families with roots in NCR.

Budget rule set by the promoter: ad spend of 0.6–0.95% of each home's value. Average home ₹3.1 Cr: base price of the 2,150 and 2,450 sq ft homes at ₹13,500 BSP, equal mix. Budget is reallocated weekly inside the approved total on cost per site visit. For context, ANAROCK says major brands keep total acquisition cost, brokerage included, at 2.5–2.8% of sales (May 2026).
